**Goal:** A gun and a thing to throw are held the way a person holds them.

Asked for by the developer in the night of Nov 13 to 14, in one message before going to sleep, with the order not to wait for a decision between steps. In the developer's words: "fix how character hold throwable items and it should look nicer", "the way holding the guns as well need to feel better".

**What is wrong now:** every gun is held between two arms stretched straight out at shoulder height. That passes for a pistol and looks wrong with a rifle 123 cm long. Everything thrown is carried overhead on two arms, a bottle like a crate.

**The plan:**
- A long gun is shouldered: the right hand at the grip by the chest, the left forward under the barrel, the gun lying along the line between them.
- A pistol is held in both hands, lower, with the elbows bent.
- A small thing (a bottle, a pan) is held in one hand, raised by the shoulder, ready to throw. A big one (a crate, a stool, a sign) stays overhead in both.
- The gun sits in the hand by its grip, not by its middle.

- [x] A crate is carried overhead in two hands: seen in the packaged game's recording of Sat Nov 14, 18:15. Before that it had not been photographed since the holds were changed
