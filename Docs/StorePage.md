## Title

Stick to the Script

## Short description

You are the stunt performer. The script says you lose. Ignore it, win the fight, then cut the film yourself.

## Description

**A playable movie for the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam. Theme: Director's Cut.**

You were hired to lose. The script says the gang beats you in the warehouse, in the bar and on the rooftop. You have other ideas.

- **No prompts and no cues.** Walk, punch, kick, duck, guard and jump. Duck and punch for an uppercut, duck and kick for a sweep, guard and punch for a hook, guard and kick for a spinning roundhouse. Jump under a red bar to hang from it, then kick off it. Throw whatever is lying on the set: crates, bottles, a watermelon, a traffic cone, a frying pan.
- **Go down and it is "Cut!"** Back to one, and every retake comes out of your fee.
- **Win and it is a wrap.** The take is played back as a film, and the cut is yours: the action cut, the slow-motion cut, or one long take.
- **The box office pays for a good show.** The same punch over and over earns nothing. Mix your moves.
- **A director with a voice**, and different things to shout in every scene.
- **Something golden** drops onto the set once in every take. Pick it up.
- **A prop shop** for what you bank: a bat, an iron bar or a sledgehammer for your next scene, and a halo, a crown or a new colour for good.
- Three scenes, five kinds of opponent, a wardrobe, and a director who cannot believe what you are doing to his stuntmen.

## How to run

Windows 10 or 11. Unzip, open the `StickToTheScript` folder and run `StickToTheScript.exe`.

## How it was made

Made during the jam, Nov 12 to 15, 2026, by one developer directing an AI coding agent (Claude Code). The agent wrote the code, drove the Unreal editor and kept a log of everything as it happened, including what went wrong. The dev log, with the scripts and the source code: https://github.com/ahmadkhalaf1/stick-to-the-script-devlog

No art was bought. The fighters' body is Mixamo's free "X Bot" model (Adobe), which the game poses itself, bone by bone, on sixteen joints. The punch, the kicks, the uppercut, the fighting stance and the way a blow is taken are free motion-capture clips from Mixamo (Adobe), fitted to that skeleton by a script. The sets, their brick, boards and concrete, and every prop are generated by scripts. The director's voice, the blows and the music were generated with ElevenLabs models through fal.ai, a partner of the jam, from words that are in the repository.
